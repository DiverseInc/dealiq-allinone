## 💡 Pricing Psychology

1. **Free tier** → Get them hooked
2. **$29/mo** → Affordable for individuals
3. **$99/mo** → No-brainer for teams
4. **Lifetime $99** → Impulse buy at launch

**Key principles:**
- Anchor with Enterprise price
- Show value per month ($0.97/day for Pro)
- Offer annual discount (2 months free)
