🚀 Getting Started

How do I create an account?
Click "Open App" and select "admin" from the dropdown. Use password: admin123. You can change this in Settings after logging in.
How do I add my first deal?
Go to "My Deals" → Click "+ Add Deal" → Fill in the property details, price, ARV, and repairs → Click Save. Your deal is now tracked!
How do I use the calculators?
Click "Calculators" in the navigation. Select a calculator, enter your numbers, and see instant results. Free users get 6 calculators; Pro users get all 13.
How do I print a form?
Go to Forms → Select a form → Fill in the details → Click "Print" button → Your browser's print dialog will open. You can save as PDF or print directly.

💰 Account & Billing

How do I upgrade to Pro?

What payment methods do you accept?

🔧 Features & Tools

What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free includes 6 calculators, basic forms, and buyer list. Pro ($29/mo) unlocks all 13 calculators, unlimited AI generators, team management, analytics, and custom workflows.
How does the Deal Score work?
The Deal Score (1-100) analyzes profit margin, ROI, deal type, cash flow, and hold time. Scores 80+ are great deals, 60-79 are good, 40-59 are fair, below 40 are poor.
How do I export my data?
Go to My Deals → Click "Export All" to download a CSV file. You can also export individual deals as text files using the PDF button.
Can I use this on my phone?
Yes! DealIQ Pro is fully responsive and works on all devices. You can also install it as a mobile app from your browser's menu.
How do I add team members?
Go to Team in the sidebar (Pro plan required). Click "+ Add Member" and enter their name, email, and role (Admin, Agent, or Assistant).

📤 Outreach & Marketing

How do I use the Direct Mail templates?
Go to Direct Mail → Select a template type (Cold, Follow-Up, Probate, etc.) → Replace [bracketed] items with your info → Click "Copy" → Paste into your mail merge software.
How do I manage my buyer list?
Go to Buyer List → Click "+ Add Buyer" → Enter their contact info, type, and budget → Save. You can search, filter, and export your list anytime.
How do I send SMS/email templates?
Go to SMS & Email → Select a template → Copy the text → Paste into your messaging app. Replace [bracketed] items with the recipient's info.
