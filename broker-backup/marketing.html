Cold Call Scripts

Initial Call

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name]. I'm reaching out because I've built a tool that's helping brokers like you save 5+ hours per week on admin tasks. It's called DealIQ Pro - it's an all-in-one platform with a visual deals pipeline, 23 AI content generators, and mortgage calculators. I'd love to give you a quick 10-minute demo. Would you be open to that this week?"

Objection Handling

If they say: "I already have a CRM" → "Great! DealIQ Pro works alongside your existing CRM. It adds AI content generation and deal pipeline features that most CRMs don't have. Would you like to see how it integrates?" If they say: "I'm too busy" → "That's exactly why this helps! It automates the repetitive tasks so you can focus on closing deals. Can I show you just the AI generators? That alone saves hours each week." If they say: "Not interested" → "No problem! If you change your mind, it's free to start at dealiqpro.com. Have a great day!"
